# Lightness and roundness experiments

Fifteen variations: five homepage heroes, five R/economics cards, and five temporary dashboard studies. Generated with the built-in image tool on 2026-10-02.

Open `index.html` to compare the images. Each caption identifies a variation. PNG files preserve the generated originals; WebP files provide quality-85 previews at 2400 × 1000 for heroes and 1600 × 1067 for cards. Exact generation prompts are in `prompts.json`.

The experiments deliberately vary the composition and the relationship between books and charts, including an emerging forecast. They retain the off-white, Yoro blue, and muted green direction, with rounded forms and generous space. Generated shades are approximate.

Review the compositions before production integration. Some books retain gentle shading, and the denser hero arrangements leave less heading space. These are schematic editorial illustrations, not instructional figures or validated empirical results. No production page references were changed.
